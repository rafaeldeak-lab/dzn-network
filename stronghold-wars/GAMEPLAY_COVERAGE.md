# Stronghold Wars gameplay coverage

This tracks the user's shared-base, clan-style and operative briefs against the **Rosebud local preview**. “Playable” means browser-saved simulation, not DZN account or server-authoritative multiplayer. Keep this distinction when publishing a game update.

| Player loop | Current preview | Remaining milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Shared server base | Playable Hall/support upgrades, member funding, owner approval, Forge, tree clearing, district claims, timed Command Compound and district construction, Level 1–5 models and completion effects. Hall tiers grant one to three builder crews; fully funded core, facility and route projects share the crew queue, show scaffolds while active, and complete once after their saved timer, including across refresh. | Reviewed DZN identity, owner Pro and community persistence service. |
| Resource production | The Workshop produces Parts and the five-level Timberline Lumber Yard separately produces Timber while each member is away. Both save a personal production clock, stop at their levelled stock caps, and pause when the community loses simulated Pro access. Returning or refreshing cannot collect the same interval twice. The Yard still gives its daily resupply. | Placeable storage buildings, richer resource sinks and authoritative server clocks. |

Patrol posts, four defenders and the animated Arc Battery protect the base. Locked wall positions intercept actual local-preview volleys. A segment disappears only when its tiered health reaches zero, and surviving segments still block the walking route. | Broader destructible cover, friendly challenge replays and persistent structure condition on a reviewed shared service. |
| Army and commander | Five upgraded ordinary squad roles plus a two-slot operative command budget; Rivet, Signal and Remedy have bounded abilities. Keep the five state layers separate: permanent community development, permanent personal records, war state, temporary assault state and seasonal competition. Cosmetics must not add competitive power. The default-off DZN bootstrap remains read-only and is not a gameplay authorization source.

## Clan-style mechanics to carry forward

The two user briefs are the feature checklist, adapted to DZN military themes and the shared Pro-community base. The playable preview currently covers collaborative funding, owner approval, Hall-gated builder construction including district facilities and routes, timed Forge work, capped offline Workshop Parts and Lumber Yard Timber production, resource pickups, clearing and claiming land, individual/row walls, saved layouts, friendly defence tests, upgraded troops, camp training, troop reinforcement requests/donations and casualty replacement, PvE raids, challenge preparation, scouts and capped war attacks with carried building and wall damage. The next gameplay milestones are placeable storage buildings, wider district destruction, scheduled multi-sector wars, and seasonal events. Level 6–10 Hall content, ranked matchmaking and real server-wide synchronization remain proposed rather than implemented. Keep permanent progression separate from seasonal score, and keep cosmetic cards out of combat stats.
